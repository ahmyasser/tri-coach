Questions

Which watches work?

Any Garmin watch that syncs to Garmin Connect. Workouts you send appear in your Garmin calendar and on the watch after its next sync.

What happens to my data?

Your training data is stored in a private section of our database, separate from everyone else's. We never sell it. Your Garmin password goes to Garmin to sign you in and is never stored. You can delete your account and all your data from Settings at any time. Details are in our privacy policy.

Is it a replacement for a human coach or doctor?

No. It's a smart training partner that's good with numbers. For injuries, illness or medical questions, see a professional.

How much does it cost?

Free during the beta. Paid plans with more coach time will come later, and beta users will hear about them first.
